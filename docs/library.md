# The book list

The list opens on the folder you used last, or on `Roms/EBOOKS` the first time. It stays on the SD card: **Up one folder** stops at the card's top folder, and a saved folder that is not on the card is ignored. The top bar names the folder and shows your place in it, such as `3 / 12`. Each epub is shown by the title and author written inside the book, such as `Dom Casmurro - Machado de Assis`, and a book without them by its file name without the extension. The titles are read in the background a few books at a time and kept in an `info` folder in the reader's store, so the list fills in quickly the first time and at once after that. The type and size are on the right, such as `EPUB, 1.2 MB`; a book you have opened also shows how far you got there, such as `42%, EPUB, 1.2 MB`, or `Done` at 99% and over. Folders sit above the books and say `Folder`. **Up one folder** is the first row inside a subfolder. If a folder has no books or folders, the list says so and tells you where to put books.

A name that is too long for its row slides once the row is selected: it waits a moment, scrolls to the end, pauses, and starts over. In the grid it slides under the selected cover.

**Select** switches between the list and the cover grid. The choice is saved. The grid shows one to four rows of covers, depending on the screen height, with the title under each cover. A book's cover is taken from the book itself (EPUB), or from a picture beside it named like the book (`Title.jpg`, `.jpeg` or `.png`) or, when the book is alone in its folder, `cover.jpg` (the Calibre layout), which also works for Markdown and text books and shrunk in the background, so the grid never waits on it. The small pictures are kept in a `covers` folder inside the reader's store, so they appear at once the next time. A book with no cover, or a `.txt` file, gets a plain colored cover with its title and type. Folders and **Up one folder** get their own tiles.

**Y** opens a small menu for the selected entry:

- **Calibre folders.** A `metadata.opf` beside a book that is alone in its folder (or `<name>.opf` beside any book) is read as well: the title, author, series and number, language, publisher, subjects, date and description in it replace what the book itself says, for epub, Markdown and text books alike.
- **Marks** in the list: a book with highlights or bookmarks shows `N marks` among its details, and **Properties** has a **Marks** row such as `5 highlights, 2 notes, 3 bookmarks`.
- **Properties** opens a page about the book: the cover, then title, author, series, language, publisher and date (epub), type, size, status, progress, the date of the file, its name and folder, and the book's own description when it has one. Up and down scroll, left and right turn a page, any other button closes it.
- **Add to favorites** or **Remove from favorites**, for a book. A favorite has `* ` in front of its name. Favorites in other folders are listed at the top of every folder, under **Continue**, so they are one press away.
- A book at 99% or more counts as finished by itself, shows **Done**, and has a **Done** label on its cover. **Status** (below) can override that either way.
- **Hide finished books** or **Show finished books**, for the whole list. Hidden books stay on the card and come back when you show them. A favorite is never hidden. The choice is saved.
- **Recent books** lists the last 40 books you opened, newest first, with the percent or Done and the date. **A** opens one.
- **Smart collections** fill themselves. In the Collections list choose **New smart collection...**, name it and add conditions one at a time: Author, Tag, Series, Language, Publisher (the text is contained in what the book says), Year (such as `1984` or `1980-1989`), Status (not started, reading, to read, on hold, finished, favorite), Type (epub, txt, md) and Rating (at least so many stars). A book must meet every condition. Start a text with `!` to mean "not", such as `!horror`. **Save and show** keeps the collection and lists the books that fit; open it again from the list to refresh it. Delete it from its Y menu; it has no Remove from collection. The conditions are kept in `smart_collections.tsv` in the store.
- **Collections** are shelves of your own. The list shows each collection and **New collection...**; **A** opens a collection as a list of its books, and the Y menu there has **Remove from collection**, **Delete collection** and **Leave collection**. **Add to collection** in the Y menu puts the selected book in one. Collections are kept in `collections.tsv` in the store, and follow a book when you rename or delete it.
- **Set progress...** (Y menu, on a book) picks a percent from 0 to 100 with the arrows. The list shows it at once, and the book opens at that place the next time, which is useful for a book you read somewhere else. Kept in `pending_progress.tsv` until the book is opened.
- **Storage** in Library tools shows what the reader's files take on the card and the free space. Covers, Book details, Page layouts, Fonts from books and the backup file can each be cleared (after a question); they are made again as needed. Places, highlights, bookmarks and your settings are shown but never cleared there.
- **Hide this folder** (Y menu on a folder) keeps a folder, and the books in it, out of the list and out of every library tool, search and count. **Library tools > Hidden folders** lists them; choose one to show it again. The folders stay on the card; the list is kept in `hidden_folders.txt` in the store.
- **Browse by publisher** and **Browse by year published** in Library tools list the books like Browse by series, from the publisher and the date in each book.
- **Group series** (Y menu, off by default) makes the volumes of a series that has two or more books in the folder one row, named after the series, with the number of books. **A** on it lists the volumes in the order of their numbers; **B** or the first row goes back. A series row has no Y menu of its own. The series of a book is known once its title has been read, so a folder collapses as it loads.
- **Status** sets a book to Automatic (by progress), To read, Reading, On hold or Finished; it replaces the old finished mark. A book you marked To read or On hold shows that in the list. **Library tools** is grouped into **Browse**, **Clean up**, **Get books** and **Back up**, with **Search inside all books** on its first screen, and holds: **Books by status** (To read, Reading, On hold, Finished and Favorites, each as a list of books), **Find copies** (books with the same name apart from case and punctuation, or the same file type and exact size, listed together so you can delete the extra one), **Books not opened yet**, **Browse by series**, **Browse by author** and **Browse by language** and **Browse by tag or genre** (the books grouped by what the book says about itself, with the number of books in each group; a series lists its books in the order of their numbers, and books that say nothing are in a last group called (none)), **Reading history** (a calendar of the month, see below), **Tidy this folder into author folders** (the loose books of the folder are moved into a folder for each author, or each author and then each series; you first see how many books go into how many folders and can cancel; books that name no author stay; reading place, status and collections move along), **Search inside all books** (type a word or phrase and the reader looks through every epub, Markdown and text book of the library, a progress bar showing the book it is in, and lists the places with the words around them and the book title under each, up to 6 places for a book and 300 in all; **A** opens that book at that place; a big library takes a while), **All highlights** (every highlight of every book you opened, newest book first, with the book under each; **Search...** on the first row narrows them by words in the highlight or the book title, and **A** opens the book at the place of the highlight; only highlights with their words kept are listed, which is every one made since the words were saved), **Back up settings and notes**, **Restore from backup** and **Get books online (OPDS)** and **Receive books over wifi**. **Receive books over wifi** starts a small web page on the handheld and shows its address, such as `http://192.168.1.50:8080`. Open it in a browser on a phone or computer on the same wifi, choose files and send them; they are saved in the **Downloads** folder of the library. Anyone on the same wifi can send files while the screen is open (only epub, Markdown and text files are kept), and it stops when you press **B**. **Get books online (OPDS)** reads an OPDS catalog (the format Calibre-web, Kavita, COPS and many free libraries serve) over wifi. It asks for the catalog address once, such as `http://192.168.1.20:8083/opds` (add `name:password@` before the host for a server that asks for a login), and keeps it. Folders of the catalog are marked `>`, books show their file type, and choosing a book saves it in a **Downloads** folder of the library; **More...** goes to the next page and **B** goes back a level. Only plain `http://` works, since the reader has no encryption, and only epub, Markdown and text files are offered. Both network parts were tested against test servers on a computer, not yet on the handheld. Also in **Library tools**: **Browse by publisher**, **Browse by year published**, **Hidden folders** (see **Hide this folder**), **Storage**, **Export all highlights and notes**, and, from the Y menu, **Collections** with its smart collections.
- **Back up settings and notes** writes `PixelReader backup.zip` in the books folder: the settings, your place in every book, the library history and statuses, collections, the word list, reading stats, and the bookmarks, highlights and layout of every book. Covers, book info and page counts are not included, because they are made again. **Restore from backup** puts the files back and then closes the reader, so open it again afterwards. Restoring replaces what is on the card now.
- **Search library** opens an on-screen keyboard. The words you type are looked for in the file name, title, author and series of every book under the top folder, in all its subfolders. A **Searching library** screen with a bar shows how far it has got. The matches replace the list, with the title and the folder they are in; **A** opens one, and **Clear search** (the first row, **B**, or the same entry in the Y menu) goes back to the folders. A search shows up to 500 books.
- **Rename file**, for a book, asks for a new name with the keyboard. The extension stays, and the progress, favorite mark, highlights and bookmarks go with the book.
- **Delete book**, for a book, asks you to confirm and then removes the file together with its progress, highlights, bookmarks and layout.
- **File management.** **Mark for copy, move or delete** puts `[x] ` in front of a book (choose **Unmark** to take it off); mark as many as you like, in one folder or several. Then **Copy marked**, **Move marked** (or **Copy this book** / **Move this book** with nothing marked) remember the books, you open the folder you want and choose **Paste here** in the Y menu. A name that is already taken gets ` (2)` added. A moved book keeps its progress, favorite mark, status, highlights, bookmarks and collections. **Delete marked** asks once and removes all of them, and **Clear marks** unmarks everything. **New folder** asks for a name with the keyboard and makes the folder in the folder you are in.
- **Next in series.** When the book you read last is finished, the list shows **Next in series: <title>** under **Continue**, if the folder holds the next number of its series. At the end of a book, pressing forward once more on the last page opens a small menu: **Next in series** (the book with the next series number in the same folder, else **Next in folder**, the next file in natural name order, where `Book 2` comes before `Book 10`), **Back to the list**, or **Stay here**.

The **on-screen keyboard** has five pages: lower case, capitals, accented lower case, accented capitals, and symbols. The D-pad moves over the keys, **A** types one, **B** deletes, **Y** types a space, **Select** (or **L1** and **R1**) changes the page, **Start** accepts, and **X** cancels.

A book you have opened has a progress bar along the bottom of its cover. In every folder except the one holding the book you read last, the first row is **Continue: <title>**, which opens that book. In the top folder, up to two more unfinished books you opened lately follow it as **Also reading: <title>**.

**Each folder keeps its own view.** Changing the order (Start), list or grid (Select) or hide-finished (Y menu) in a subfolder is remembered for that folder only, in `folders.tsv` in the store. The top folder sets the default for folders that have no choice of their own, as before. **Use the default view in this folder** in the Y menu clears a folder's choice.

**Start** changes the order of the books: by file name (the default), by title, by author, by series (series name, then the number in the series), most recently read first, largest first, newest file first (the date the file was put on the card), or most read first (books in progress, the furthest along first, then books not opened, then finished ones). Folders always stay first, and the order is saved. The folder name in the top bar says which order is active.

| Button | In the book list and the grid |
| --- | --- |
| D-pad up / down | List: move one row. Grid: move one row of covers. Hold to keep moving. |
| D-pad left / right | List: move half a screen. Grid: move one cover. |
| L1 / R1 | Move half a screen in the list, or a full screen of covers in the grid. |
| A | Open the book or folder. |
| Y | The options of the selected entry: properties, favorite, finished, hide finished, **Show** (switches which books the folder lists: all, not opened, in progress, favorites, finished), search, rename, delete. |
| Select | Switch between the list and the grid. |
| Start | Change the order: name, title, author, series, recent. |
| X | Settings. |
| B | Go up one folder. In the top folder it does nothing. |
| Menu | Quit the app. |
